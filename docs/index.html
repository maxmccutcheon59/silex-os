Silex

Authorization and audit for robots and agents.

Silex is a kernel that decides whether a machine is allowed to act, runs the next legal step, and records what happened. Models may propose work. They do not execute it.

How a command moves

ProposeHuman or model drafts steps
WritCapability for actor, action, resource
ExecuteAdapter talks to a cell or log
ProveHash-chained ledger entry
CloseOr halt and roll state back

OEM safety systems stay in the loop. Silex is not a motion controller and not a safety PLC.

What exists now

KernelJobs, writs, graph, transactional rollback, hash-chained ledger
AdaptersSimulated kitting cell; recorded controller-log replay
IntelligenceAllow-listed planner; optional LLM proposer; agents cannot skip writs
ConsoleLocal operator board on 127.0.0.1
ProofConformance tests and JSON ledger export
