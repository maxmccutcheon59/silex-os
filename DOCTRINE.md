# Doctrine

## Company

Become the default layer other software and robots must use to change the world.
Windows of authorized action. CUDA of the commit.

## Quality

Hold the plot. Ask one question when blocked. Finish or halt with a reason a plant can use.
Never close a job you did not finish.

## Equity

Software margins. No metal. No lab-first raise. Dual-class when incorporated. Revenue before a priced round.

## Kernel laws

1. No action without a live writ.
2. A writ is a capability, not a slogan. Actor + actions + resource + expiry + revoke.
3. The ledger is append-only and hash-chained.
4. Adapters execute. The kernel authorizes and records.
5. Quality failure is a halt, not a close.

## Refuse

- Frontier LLM as the company identity
- Financing robots
- Copilot seats as the product
- Closing a work order the runtime did not complete
