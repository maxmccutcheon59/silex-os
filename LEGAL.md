A paid pilot requires a separate written agreement covering:

- site, cell, and permitted actors

- prohibition on using Silex as the sole safety system
- insurance and indemnity

## Agents and models

Language models and software agents are actors. They may propose steps. They may not execute physical or plant-changing actions unless the kernel has issued a writ to a *separate* executing actor (cell, arm, AMR, human). Model output is untrusted input.

## Traces

Do not put secrets, badge IDs, or personal data into goals or logs. Plant traces are customer data. The kernel retains only what a written contract allows.
